We're excited to join the October 2026 Mayo Clinic Platform Accelerate cohort.

CardioAI is advancing AI agents for ECG, cardiac MRI and echocardiography, a women-specific cardiovascular risk model, and AI-powered CVD risk calculators, to help clinicians recognize heart disease earlier.

Focus area

Heart disease is often found late.

We're advancing tools that read the signals clinicians already collect, from ECG, cardiac MRI and echocardiography to the risk factors in a patient's record, to flag patients who may need a closer look. The aim is to give care teams more time to act.

Through this program, we aim to further develop, validate and scale our approach, working with Mayo Clinic Platform and leveraging its resources as part of the cohort.

Main Engine

ECG, cardiac MRI and echocardiography

AI agents designed to help clinicians recognize heart attacks, rhythm disorders and changes in heart muscle function.

Women CVD

A women-specific risk model

Addressing heart conditions often under-recognised in women, including SCAD, peripartum cardiomyopathy, Takotsubo syndrome and cardiovascular risk after preeclampsia.

Risk calculators

AI-powered CVD risk tools

Helping clinicians identify patients at risk of heart attack, stroke, heart failure, sudden cardiac arrest and atrial fibrillation earlier.
