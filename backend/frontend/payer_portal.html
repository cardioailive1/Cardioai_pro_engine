Controlled Estimate (with a control group) matched difference-in-differences — gives a number only if it passes its checks

The estimate above has no control group, so it cannot show the program caused a change. This one compares members who were reached with similar members who were not, and reports the difference in how their cost per member per month changed. Upload one row per member. Required columns: member_id, treated, pre_pmpm, post_pmpm (treated is 1 or 0; use paid, not billed, cost). Every other numeric column (for example age, risk_score) is used to match, and must be measured before the program started. If members were chosen by a risk-score cutoff, the groups may not overlap and this will decline to give a number. A randomized holdout is the strongest design.
